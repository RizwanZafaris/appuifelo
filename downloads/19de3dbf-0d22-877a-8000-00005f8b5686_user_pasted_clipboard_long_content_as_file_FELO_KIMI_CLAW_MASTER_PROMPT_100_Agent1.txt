WHAT FELO IS
FELO is a budgeting and money-orchestration app for South Asian immigrants and cross-border households.
Primary corridor: Canada ↔ Pakistan (CAD ↔ PKR)
Secondary corridors: India · Bangladesh · Nepal · Sri Lanka
3 Rules That Never Change
1.  No-custody. FELO never holds customer funds. Licensed partners (MSB/NBFC/EMI) execute. FELO reads, plans, and tracks.
2.  SMS Parser is the moat. On-device parsing of Easypaisa, JazzCash, HBL, UBL, MCB, Meezan (PK), UPI (IN), bKash/Nagad (BD). Zero raw SMS leaves the device.
3.  Earn-Your-Premium is the business model. Users earn Pro access by completing remittances or bill payments. FELO earns 3.7–6.7× more from earned-premium users than from paid subscribers.
